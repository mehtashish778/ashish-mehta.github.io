About

I am a Research Engineer at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), working on medical AI. My research centres on vision-language models for 3D CT and MR: models that classify, write reports, answer questions, ground findings and segment, together with the evaluation needed to show they are reliable.

Alongside that, I work on parameter-efficient adaptation of frozen medical models, learning from radiology reports instead of dense annotations, ontology-guided clinical agents, and efficient inference for clinical language models. Most of this work is in progress or under review, so I describe it here by topic.

Before MBZUAI I worked at the iACE Lab, IIT Madras, on the iSafe process-safety platform (P&ID digitisation, knowledge graphs and agentic hazard analysis), and consulted on a production AI film pipeline on AWS. I like deterministic orchestration around models, explicit cost guardrails and measurable evaluation, whatever the domain.

Now & Next

Working on now

  • One model for CT and MR: classification, report writing, VQA, 3D grounding and promptable segmentation in a single set of weights, without losing segmentation quality.
  • Learning from reports: lesion segmentation trained from radiology reports instead of dense masks.
  • Efficient adaptation: compact concept and evidence adapters as an alternative to QLoRA on frozen medical VLMs.
  • Verified grounded dialogue about medical images, with every answer checkable against the image.
  • Clinical agents: ontology-guided patient intake that asks only what the record does not yet know, and real-time voice agents with event-aware noise isolation.
  • Efficient clinical LLMs: transferring KV caches between models while preserving critical clinical facts.

Where I am heading

  • Medical foundation models for 3D imaging that reason, ground and segment, trained with reinforcement learning on verifiable rewards.
  • Rigorous, leakage-controlled evaluation: patient-level splits, bootstrapped confidence, clear limits on clinical claims.
  • Cheaper, smaller and safer adaptation of large models for hospitals and low-resource settings.
  • Agents that are auditable in clinical workflows, with privacy and infrastructure built in.
  • Publishing the research, and shipping the engineering behind it.
