About

I am a Research Engineer at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), working on medical vision-language models (VLMs). My work spans the whole training stack: data acquisition and preparation, architecture-level changes, pre-training, supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and RL-based fine-tuning, run on large multi-node GPU clusters.

My earlier work at IIT Madras was in industrial AI and safety-critical systems: digitising process diagrams and automating hazard studies for chemical plants, and building and deploying ML software into an air-gapped industrial R&D environment. I care about systems that are reproducible, deployable and measurable, whatever the domain.

Now & Next

Working on now

  • Medical VLMs: pre-training and SFT on large-scale medical data, including 3D CT and MR.
  • RL fine-tuning: reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards for grounding and segmentation.
  • Data engines: acquisition, curation and preparation of large training corpora.
  • Architecture: changes to model components, and parameter-efficient alternatives to full fine-tuning.
  • Evaluation: patient-level splits, leakage control and clear limits on clinical claims.

Where I am heading

  • Medical foundation models for 3D imaging that reason, ground and segment.
  • Cheaper, smaller and safer adaptation of large models for hospitals and low-resource settings.
  • Auditable clinical agents with privacy and infrastructure built in.
  • Publishing the research and shipping the engineering behind it.
