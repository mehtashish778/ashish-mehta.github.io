About

I am a Research Engineer at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), where I compare retrieval-augmented avatar systems and explore how vision-language models can be combined with graph reasoning.

Before that I worked at the iACE Lab, IIT Madras, on the iSafe process-safety platform: digitising P&ID diagrams, building knowledge-graph pipelines and designing agentic workflows for hazard analysis. I also consulted on a production AI film pipeline on AWS.

My work sits between applied research and production engineering. I like deterministic orchestration around LLMs, explicit cost guardrails and measurable evaluation, so that a system can be trusted and not just demoed.
